# com_biotime 0.9.0

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install the attached package. No database changes.

- **My Dashboard shows the school year so far**, from 1 August to today (before August, from
  1 August of last year). There is no month selection any more; the range is shown under the
  heading and every card opens My Attendance for the same dates.
- **Under the heading**: department · position · employee ID · name.
- **Cards, 5 per row**, now with **Holidays** and **Weekend Days Worked** (and Overtime as its own card).
- **Monthly Overview and Your Devices are no longer on My Dashboard.** Your devices are on My Punches.
- **My Attendance: From and To dates** below the month, to show any range up to a year. Choosing a
  month or year clears them.
