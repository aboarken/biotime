# com_biotime 0.6.3 — Late In and Early Out from ZKBioTime 9.5

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install the attached package. No database changes.

- **Fixed: Late In and Early Out were empty.** ZKBioTime 9.5 saves its calculation in the time card and
  pay code tables; the portal now reads them there: Late In (L), Early Out (E), Worked Hours, Absence,
  HOLIDAY, WEEKEND and leave. Nothing is calculated by the portal: a day shows its figures after
  ZKBioTime's calculation (Auto Calculate, e.g. 21:00).
- **Leave days** show the leave's name (e.g. Annual Leave) instead of Absent.
- **Holidays are purple**; Weekend Days Worked is sky blue.
