# com_biotime 0.4.6 — faster pages

- **Fixed: every page slow** when ZKBioTime did not answer the once-a-day employee check: the check
  was retried, and waited for, on every page load. It is now tried again after an hour.
- **Past months kept for 24 hours**: going back to a past month, and My Punches, no longer ask
  ZKBioTime again after the first read of the day. The current month still refreshes after the
  Cache time (default 5 minutes), so new punches show up as quickly as before.
- **Saved data in its own table** (`#__biotime_cache`) instead of the session: it survives logging
  out and does not slow other pages of the site. Old entries are deleted automatically.
- **One connection per page** to ZKBioTime instead of a new one for every request.

Updating creates the new table. No settings change; keep *Cache time* at 5 minutes.
