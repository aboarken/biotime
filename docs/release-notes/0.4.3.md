# com_biotime 0.4.3 — check-in before 12:00, check-out from 12:00, date picker

- **Check-in and check-out by the time of day**: the first punch before 12:00 is the check-in, the
  last punch from 12:00 on is the check-out, whatever the device recorded. An afternoon-only punch
  is now shown under Check-Out (check-in *Missing*).
- **Checked In ↘ / Checked Out ↗** for today, with the same arrows as the times, also in the
  calendar legend.
- **Date picker** on My Attendance (DD/MM/YYYY); choosing a day filters at once.
- **Page titles** use " - " instead of "|".
- **Devices You Used** on My Dashboard: fingerprint, face and total per device, with totals.
- **Recent Activity**: a Day column (e.g. Sunday).

No database or settings changes.
