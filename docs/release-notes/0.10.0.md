# com_biotime 0.10.0

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install the attached package. No database changes.

- **Statistics on My Dashboard** (replaces Recent Activity): colourful charts of the cards'
  figures: a donut of the days so far, days per month, late in and early out per month, average
  check-in and check-out per month, and circles for missing punches, weekend work and late or
  early days. Hover any bar, slice or point to see its value.
- **Your details as a table row** under the heading: Department, Position, Employee ID, Name and
  Period (1 August to today).
- **A plainer note** under the pages explaining where the figures come from and how check-in and
  check-out are chosen.
- The portal pages now say **the Attendance Management System** instead of ZKBioTime: the note
  under the pages, error messages and the other notes employees see. The administrator pages
  still name ZKBioTime.
- **My Attendance shows every day of a From – To range on one page**, with no further pages.
