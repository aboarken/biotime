# com_biotime 0.4.2 — Missing Punch, My Punches, DD/MM/YYYY

## What's new

- **Missing Punch.** A day with any punch is **Present**. If its check-in or check-out is missing,
  the day also shows an amber **Missing Punch** tag and *Missing* under the missing time. The first
  punch counts as the check-in, even when it is the only one; a lone check-out punch stays a
  check-out. The Monthly Overview counts such a day as Present and as Missing Punch.
- **My Punches** (replaces Biometric Devices): your own punches for a year or one month: by
  fingerprint and by face, per month with totals, and per device.
- **My Dashboard**: 10 cards (6, then 4); **My Attendance**: 8 cards (5, then 3); all cards of a
  page are the same size. Devices You Used lists every device used in the month.
- **Dates as DD/MM/YYYY**, also in the date filter. Year lists start at 2025.
- **Leave** is no longer shown.

## After updating

Biometric Devices menu items now open My Punches, and the groups allowed to view Biometric Devices
may view My Punches. Check *Components → ZKBioTime → Options → Permissions → View My Punches* and
allow it for the employees' group (e.g. Registered) if everyone should see their own punches.

No database schema changes.
